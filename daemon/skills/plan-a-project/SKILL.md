# Plan a project

The goal is a plan the person can start on today: clear about what done means, ordered, and
honest about what is not known yet.

## Steps

1. **Say the goal in one sentence**, as a result someone could check: "The shop takes card
   payments on the phone", not "improve payments". If the person's words do not give you that,
   ask one question.
2. **Collect the limits**: the date, the people, the money, the tools that are fixed, what must
   not change. Ask only for the ones that would change the plan.
3. **Define done**: three to five things that are true when the project is finished. Each one can
   be checked with a yes or a no.
4. **List what is not part of it.** Two or three things people would expect but that are left
   out. This saves the most time later.
5. **Break it into stages**, three to six. Each stage ends with something that works or can be
   shown, not with "research done".
6. **Break the first stage into steps.** Each step:
   - starts with a verb;
   - takes between half an hour and a day;
   - has one clear result;
   - names what it needs from an earlier step.
   Leave later stages as one line each. They will change.
7. **Order by risk, then by need.** What might not work at all comes early, in its smallest form.
   What others wait for comes before what nobody waits for.
8. **Name the unknowns.** For each: what you do not know, and the cheapest way to find out. An
   unknown that could stop the project becomes step one.
9. **Check the plan**: does doing every step give every point of "done"? Is there a step no point
   needs? Remove it.

## Rules

- Use the person's own words for their things.
- No dates or hours unless the person gave some to work from. Use small, medium, large for size.
- Do not plan what you cannot know yet. Say "decide after stage 1".
- Keep it to one screen. A plan nobody reads is no plan.
- If the goal is too large for one plan, plan the first useful piece and say what comes after.

## The answer

- **Goal**: one sentence.
- **Done when**: the checks.
- **Not part of this**: the short list.
- **Stages**: numbered, one line each, with what it ends with.
- **First stage, step by step**: numbered steps with size (S, M, L).
- **Unknowns**: each with how to find out.
- **Start with**: the one step to do first, and why.
