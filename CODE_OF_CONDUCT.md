# Code of conduct

Rigbrew is built in the open by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. Be the kind
of person you would want to meet in an issue thread.

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention, in any form.
- Help newcomers. A question that seems simple to you is a reason to answer kindly.
- Keep discussions on topic and in good faith.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block people who
break them repeatedly. To report a problem privately, use GitHub's private reporting on this repository or contact
the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
