# Before you start

Version: draft-2026-11-20.2

Rigbrew runs AI models on your own computer. Your prompts are not sent to any model provider.

Rigbrew itself collects data in two layers and sends it to Rigbrew's server.

**What you write and what the models answer, if you agree.** The full text of your prompts and of the responses,
and the prompts you use for pictures, linked to your Rigbrew account (anonymous until you sign in). Generated
pictures, audio and video stay on your computer. We use this to see where the models fail, to improve the model
builds, and to train and evaluate models. Do not type anything into Rigbrew that you would not want stored on a
server, including personal information about other people.

**How the app is used, always.** Screens opened, buttons used for the main actions, models downloaded, started and
removed, how fast they ran, how long things took, errors, your operating system and a hardware class (processor,
memory and graphics, grouped; no serial numbers), the app version and a random installation ID. This never
contains what you type or what the models answer, and it is not linked to your account. It is sent whether or not
you agree to the first layer, so we know how many people use Rigbrew and what works.

You can see what is waiting to be sent in **Settings → Privacy and data**. You can withdraw your agreement to the
first layer there at any time: content that has not been sent yet is removed from this computer and no new content
is collected; basic usage data continues. You can ask for your data to be exported or deleted from **Settings →
Account**.

By choosing **Accept and continue** you confirm that you are at least 18 years old and that you agree to the
collection of what you write and what the models answer as described above, to the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md)
and to the [Terms of Use](terms.md).
